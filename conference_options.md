## 1. VISAPP 2027 — International Conference on Computer Vision Theory and Applications

**Where/when:** Valletta, Malta — 26–28 February 2027
**Deadline:** Two submission stages. First stage: 15 September 2026 (earlier notification). Second stage (still regular papers, not just late/position papers): **22 October 2026**, notification 4 December 2026.
**Domain fit:** Excellent. Explicit tracks include Recognition & Detection, Deep Learning for Visual Understanding, Low-Level Vision & Computational Imaging — directly the subject area of your garment-detection pipeline.
**Indexing:** Proceedings submitted for evaluation to **Scopus, DBLP, Web of Science (Conference Proceedings Citation Index), Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar**. Not IEEE — published via SCITEPRESS — but a shortlist of extended papers is typically offered a slot in a **Springer CCIS** book (final approval pending each year).
**Age:** 22nd edition. The series traces back to roughly 2006 — comfortably over your 10-year floor.
**International character:** Organized by INSTICC (Portugal), rotating European venues, genuinely global program committee (this edition chaired by University of Barcelona and University of Catania).

**The actual gap:** Deadline is late October, not Nov/Dec. If you can realistically finish the paper by then, this is probably your strongest overall option.

**Link:** https://visapp.scitevents.org/

## 2. ICPRS 2027 — International Conference on Pattern Recognition Systems

**Where/when:** Bordeaux, France — 8–11 March 2027
**Deadline:** 25 October 2026
**Domain fit:** Strong — Pattern Recognition Systems, endorsed by IAPR (International Association for Pattern Recognition), which also runs ICPR, the field's flagship venue. Broad enough to cover a garment-detection + compatibility-scoring pipeline.
**Indexing:** Site states papers "deemed to be of the required standard AND presented at the conference are expected to be published by the IEEE and indexed in IEEE Xplore." This is the same standard conditional language IEEE uses for essentially every one of its co-sponsored conferences (quality-check-dependent) — not a special red flag like ICACCS's publisher switch, just the normal caveat.
**Age:** 16th edition. Traces back to the Chilean Conference on Pattern Recognition, which reached its 6th edition in 2014 — meaning the series root goes back to roughly 2009. About 18 years old by 2027.
**International character:** Organized jointly by Université de Bordeaux (France) and the Chilean Association for Pattern Recognition (ACHiRP), under IAPR endorsement. Genuinely international committee and rotating host institutions.

**The actual gap:** Same as VISAPP — deadline is late October, not Nov/Dec. Indexing is "expected," not locked in yet for this specific edition (normal for IEEE conferences at this stage).

**Link:** https://www.icprs.org/

## 3. ICACCS 2027 — International Conference on Advanced Computing and Communication Systems

**Where/when:** Sri Eshwar College of Engineering, Coimbatore, India — likely March 2027 (past editions have consistently landed in March, but 2027 dates aren't confirmed yet)
**Deadline:** Not yet posted. Submissions open 6 August 2026; based on past years the deadline has historically landed around December, but that's an inference, not a confirmed date for this edition.
**Domain fit:** Strong — explicitly lists Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning as a named research area.
**Indexing:** The actual problem here. Earlier editions (e.g. 2025) went to IEEE Xplore. The 2027 edition's own site now says accepted papers go to the **SCITEPRESS Digital Library ("Waiting for Approval")**, with Scopus/EI Compendex described as **"under evaluation."** This is a real change from the series' own history, not just unconfirmed-by-me boilerplate — email the organizers for written confirmation before submitting if indexing is the actual point of doing this.
**Age:** 12th edition, running since roughly 2016 — passes the 10-year bar, though less comfortably than the other three.
**International character:** "International" in the title, India-organized and India-hosted, not a globally-rotating venue.

**Link:** https://icaccs.sece.ac.in/index.html

## 4. ICIPCN 2027 — International Conference on Image Processing and Capsule Networks

**Where/when:** G.T.N. College of Technology, Dindigul, India — 21–23 January 2027
**Deadline:** 29 October 2026
**Domain fit:** The strongest of all four, by a wide margin. This conference's entire scope *is* image processing, computer vision, pattern recognition, and deep learning (with a specific emphasis on Capsule Networks, though general image processing work is explicitly welcomed too). Your garment-detection-to-embeddings pipeline sits naturally here — closer than any of the other three.
**Indexing:** Confirmed — all registered and presented papers go into **IEEE Xplore**. No ambiguity like ICACCS has this cycle.
**Age:** The actual problem. This is only the **7th edition**, with the series starting around 2020 — roughly 7 years old by 2027. Falls short of your stated 10-year minimum. If that rule is hard (institutional/scholarship requirement), this one is disqualified regardless of fit.
**International character:** "International" in the title, India-organized (G.T.N. College of Technology) and India-hosted, though the technical program committee does include a genuinely international mix of academics (Czech Republic, Romania, Taiwan, Thailand, Portugal, Greece, Canada, etc.) — broader international reach on the committee than ICACCS or INDIACom had.

**Link:** https://icipcn.com/2027/

## Recommendation logic

- **If Nov/Dec deadline is the one non-negotiable constraint:** none of these four actually lands there — all sit in October. You'd need to reconsider that constraint or keep watching for a later-posted CFP.
- **If genuine international standing + strongest domain fit matter most:** VISAPP is the top pick — best combination of fit, indexing breadth, and track record, only ~6 weeks earlier than your target deadline.
- **If the 10-year age rule has any flexibility:** ICIPCN is the best paper-to-venue match of all four — your work won't be forced into a track it doesn't belong in.
- **Avoid committing to ICACCS** until you get written confirmation of this year's actual publisher/indexing outcome.
- Keep an eye on https://aideadlines.org — new CFPs for early-2027 conferences keep getting posted through late 2026, so a better exact-fit option could still surface.
