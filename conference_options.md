## 1. INDIACom 2027

**Dates:** 7–9 April 2027, Bharati Vidyapeeth, New Delhi
**Deadline:** Full paper submission — 7 December 2026
**Series age:** 14th–20th-ish edition depending how you count, running since 2007. Comfortably clears the 10-year bar.
**Indexing:** Has applied for IEEE's Conference Publication Program — papers submitted for inclusion in **IEEE Xplore** (subject to their quality check), and the conference itself is indexed in **ISI, Scopus, DBLP, EI-Compendex, Google Scholar**. Selected papers can also be extended into a Springer Nature journal (International Journal of Information Technology, BJIT).

**Domain fit — the actual problem:** I checked the official 2027 theme page directly. The four tracks are:
1. Sustainable Computing
2. High Performance Computing
3. High Speed Networking and Information Security
4. Software Engineering and Emerging Technologies

There is **no explicit AI/ML or image-processing/computer-vision track.** Your paper would have to be positioned under "Emerging Technologies" or argued into "Sustainable Computing" (e.g. framing the stylist app as reducing fashion overconsumption/waste). It's not a natural fit on paper — doable with the right framing, but you're working against the grain of what the conference is actually built around.

**Link:** https://indiacom.org/Home/Proceedings

## 2. ICACCS 2027 (International Conference on Advanced Computing and Communication Systems)

**Dates:** Not yet finalized for 2027; the last several editions have consistently landed in March (2025 was March 21–22), so a March 2027 date is likely but not confirmed.
**Deadline:** Not yet posted. Submissions open 6 August 2026 — historically the deadline has landed around December, but that's an inference from past years, not a confirmed 2027 date.
**Series age:** 12th edition in 2027, running since roughly 2016. Passes the 10-year bar, though less comfortably than INDIACom.
**Domain fit:** Strong — explicitly lists Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning as a named research area, alongside Computer Science, IT, and related fields. This is a direct match for your capstone.

**Indexing — the actual problem:** This is where it gets risky. Earlier editions of ICACCS (e.g. 2025) were submitted to **IEEE Xplore**. The 2027 edition's own site now says accepted papers will go into the **SCITEPRESS Digital Library ("Waiting for Approval")**, with Scopus/EI Compendex indexing described as "under evaluation" — not confirmed. That's a real change in publisher from what the series has historically used, and it's not yet locked in. If a Scopus/IEEE-indexed publication is the actual goal (not just "a conference"), **email the organizers before submitting** to get written confirmation of indexing status for this specific edition.

**Link:** https://icaccs.sece.ac.in/index.html

## 3. ICIPCN 2027 (International Conference on Image Processing and Capsule Networks)

**Dates:** 21–23 January 2027, G.T.N. College of Technology, Dindigul, India
**Deadline:** Full paper submission — 29 October 2026 (a little earlier than your "Nov end/Dec" target, but in the same general window)
**Domain fit:** By far the strongest of the three. This conference's entire scope *is* image processing, computer vision, pattern recognition, and deep learning (with a specific emphasis on Capsule Networks, though general image processing work is explicitly welcomed too). Your garment-detection-to-embeddings pipeline would sit naturally here.
**Indexing:** Confirmed — all registered and presented papers go into **IEEE Xplore**. No ambiguity like ICACCS has this cycle.

**Age — the actual problem:** This is only the **7th edition**, with the series starting around 2020. That's roughly 7 years old by 2027, which falls short of your stated 10-year minimum. If that rule is a hard requirement (e.g. for an institutional scholarship, résumé credibility threshold, or department policy), this one is disqualified outright regardless of how good the fit is otherwise.

**Link:** https://icipcn.com/2027/
